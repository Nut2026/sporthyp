## About Sporthyp

Each run is one unique match broken into five psychological states. Sporthyp is a 5–10 minute narrative-driven web experience: an audio-psychological simulation of sports obsession.

Time pressure, neo-brutalism, black-and-white: the high at its purest. 

## Features

### Phase 1: The Spark
- Enter your name, your country, the opponent, match name
- Headlines raining down as push notifications
- Media feed with climbing reactions, ageing timestamps, and clickable counters
- Countdown before tickets sell out: miss it and the cycle restarts
- $2,000 virtual economy with an ATM that pays out $5 at a time

### Phase 2: The Scarcity Hunt
- 100-seat map where green seats turn red in real time
- Bot check with a 10-second timer, then the "Loading Queue" panic
- 50% chance of "Our servers are experiencing intense traffic"

### Phase 3: The Gateway
- Merch grid: themed items with stock sold off live
- Choose your commute: Self-Drive ($50, slow) or Official Shuttle ($150, dedicated lane)
- Concourse queue and a ticket scanner to align — verified unlocks the stands
- Countdown decides if you're too late: **BANNED FROM ENTRY**
- Stadium snacks: goodies that sell, fast

### Phase 4: The War
- Event commentary with a live scoreboard updating per event
- Cheerboard: ROAR | CHANT | BOO | GASP | GROAN

### Phase 5: The Crash & The Fade
- Post-match reaction feed
- "The high has passed." - and the button to do it all again
